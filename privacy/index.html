Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy describes what Horizen collects and how it is used. It applies to the bot and this website.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Horizen handles when it is added to a Discord server or when you use its commands and website.

2. Information we handle

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs and role IDs needed to run features.
  • Server settings: prefixes, welcome and leave messages, autoroles, whitelists, logging and AutoMod configuration.
  • Feature data: warnings and moderator notes, leveling and rank data, playlists and favorites, tickets, custom commands and similar content you create with the bot.
  • Message content: processed when needed to run commands, AutoMod and logging features you enable. [state whether you store message content, and for how long]
  • Premium orders: the Discord username or ID and server ID you submit when ordering Premium, and payment details handled by your payment provider.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as browser type, as is standard for any website. [list any analytics you use, or say none]

3. How we use information

  • To provide and improve Horizen features.
  • To enforce moderation and security settings configured by server staff.
  • To fulfil Premium orders and give support.
  • To detect abuse and keep the service secure.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties except where needed to operate the service (for example, hosting, music sources and payment providers), where required by law, or to protect the service and its users. [list your main processors]

5. Retention and deletion

Server data is kept while Horizen is in your server so features keep working. [state how long data is kept after the bot is removed] You can ask us to delete data linked to your server or account by contacting us.

6. Your choices

  • Server staff can disable modules, clear data or remove the bot at any time.
  • You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data we hold, but no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

Horizen is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.
